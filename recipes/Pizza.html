Pizza

Description

At its core, the classic recipe relies on a well-fermented dough made from flour, water, yeast, and salt, which is stretched thin and baked at a high temperature to achieve a crisp yet chewy crust. This foundational base is traditionally topped with a vibrant, seasoned tomato sauce and melted mozzarella cheese, creating a perfect balance of savory, sweet, and rich flavors.

Ingredients

  • flour
  • water
  • salt
  • olive oil for dough
  • crushed tomatoes for the sauce
  • mozzarella cheese

Steps:

    Make the dough

  1. Mix 1 cup of warm water, 0.25 oz active dry yeast, and 1 tsp sugar. Let it sit for 10 minutes.
  2. Add 2.5 cups bread flour, 2 tbsp olive oil, and 1 tsp salt. Mix and let it rest for 5 minutes.

    3. Shape the Crust

  3. Roll the dough into a 12-inch circle on a floured surface.
  4. Leave the outer edges slightly thicker to form the raised crust border.

    5. Add Toppings

  5. Spread a thin layer of tomato or marinara sauce over the middle of the dough.
  6. Add a moderate amount of shredded mozzarella cheese and your favorite toppings.

    7. Bake the Pizza

  7. Preheat your oven to 450 degrees Fahrenheit.
  8. Bake the pizza for 15 to 20 minutes until the crust is golden and the cheese bubbles.
