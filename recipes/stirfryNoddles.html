Stirfry Noddles

Description

A classic, versatile Asian dish made by quickly cooking noodles with a variety of fresh ingredients in a hot wok or skillet. The foundation typically consists of wheat, rice, or egg noodles tossed alongside crisp vegetables like bell peppers, cabbage, and carrots, often accompanied by proteins such as chicken, beef, tofu, or shrimp.

Ingredients

  • 6 ounces noodles (ramen, lo mein, or rice noodles)
  • 2 cups sliced vegetables (carrots, bell peppers, broccoli, or cabbage)
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 3 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon sesame oil

Steps:

  1. Boil the noodles in a pot of water until soft, then drain and rinse with cold water.
  2. Heat oil in a large pan or wok over medium-high heat.
  3. Add the minced garlic and sliced vegetables to the hot pan.
  4. Cook and stir the vegetables for 4 to 5 minutes until tender.
  5. Add the cooked noodles, soy sauce, and sesame oil to the vegetables.
  6. Toss everything together for 1 to 2 minutes until hot and well mixed.
